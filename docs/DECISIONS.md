# Decisions

## Established decisions
- Candidate-first product direction is the governing product objective.
- The recruiter workflow is no longer the primary product direction.
- Company-specific interviewing remains a core product feature.
- Interview Profile is a central domain concept.
- Interview stage and interview nature are distinct concepts.
- The product will use a hybrid knowledge model combining internal technical knowledge and company-specific knowledge.
- Live research is part of the product direction when company knowledge is missing or stale.
- Evidence and provenance matter; recommendations, scores, and reports should be traceable.
- The existing core interview/RAG/backend functionality should be preserved where it aligns with the new direction.
- Product work should proceed in vertical slices with durable handoff/state between phases.
